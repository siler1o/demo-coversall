What we do

Ten scopes, from roof to floor

One contractor for the building's skin and its interior finishes. Point at a scope to find it on the section.

  1. ACP cladding & facade works

    Supply and installation of aluminium composite panel cladding and architectural facade finishes, to approved drawings and manufacturer standards.

  2. Drywall, partition & ceiling systems

    Non-load-bearing drywall partitions, metal furring and suspended ceilings, including gypsum board and finishing.

  3. Glass & aluminium systems

    Aluminium framing, windows, doors, shopfronts and architectural glazing. Structural curtain wall engineering only when specified.

  4. Architectural paint & protective coatings

    Interior and exterior paint and coating systems, including surface preparation and finishing.

  5. Insulation systems

    Thermal, acoustic and PU insulation for walls, ceilings, roofs and mechanical applications.

  6. Architectural fit-outs

    Raw interior to ready-for-use: partitions, flooring, ceilings, lighting, fixtures and the final finishing touches.

  7. Custom kitchen cabinetry & built-in joinery

    Modular and custom kitchen cabinets and built-in furniture for homes and commercial interiors.

  8. Roof works

    Supply and installation, from material selection to execution on site.

  9. Stone works

    Granite, quartz and engineered stone for floors, walls, countertops and architectural details.

  10. Floor finishes

    Polished concrete, epoxy and resin-based floor coatings for commercial and industrial use.

How we work

Every job runs on documents, not guesswork

Projects are delivered based on documented requirements, cost parameters and actual site conditions.

  1. 01

    Scope & drawings

    We review the approved plans, specifications and contractual scope, and flag constructability issues early.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    Our estimator prices the work against the documents, the agreed cost parameters and what we find on site.

  3. 03

    Method & schedule

    Method statements and a sequenced schedule, coordinated with your project team before mobilisation.

  4. 04

    Controlled execution

    Supervisors and trade personnel on site, with internal checks on workmanship, material usage and safety.

  5. 05

    Finish

    Works completed to the drawings and handed over. Prepared to finish is our motto, and our standard.

Company

A contracting partner for repeat and long-term projects

CoversAll Corporation is a contracting company engaged in architectural finishing and building envelope works. Our work is carried out by experienced supervisors and trade personnel with direct site exposure across residential, commercial and industrial projects.

Mission. To deliver construction works in accordance with approved plans, specifications and contractual scope, using practical engineering methods, controlled processes and accountable site management, on time and within agreed cost parameters.

Vision. To be a reliable contracting partner recognised for disciplined execution, technical understanding of finishing systems, and consistent performance across repeat and long-term projects.
